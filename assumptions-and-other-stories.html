Short Story Collection · Kirkus Reviewed

Assumptions and Other Stories

Short fiction concerned with interior lives and observation — stories that turn on what people assume about one another, and how often those assumptions are wrong. The collection opens with “Assumptions,” a story of a family beach trip and the quiet judgments that shape it.

Praised for the “storytelling prowess of the contemporary Irish masters” (Readers' Favorite).

"The quieter scenes show the mind of a talented writer at work… Mulhern demonstrates considerable powers of description, particularly when portraying his saddest characters." — Kirkus Reviews
