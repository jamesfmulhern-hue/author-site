Downloadable Studies

Typeset, house-style PDF editions of the critical and craft studies — print-ready companions to the essays, free to download. Read them alongside the pages they grew from, or on their own.

One Habit, Two Theologies

Mulhern’s craft read against the doctrine of Flannery O’Connor’s Mystery and Manners — the shared premise, the shared comic-terrible, and the fork where certainty and mercy part. Read the essay · Download the PDF

Conversion Without Erasure

A close reading of “Assumptions” — how stain becomes stigmata, baldness a crown, and the sacred enters through ordinary nouns while keeping a mundane explanation in the same clause. Read the essay · Download the PDF

The Remembering Voice

Narrative style in the short fiction — the child’s sensory filter held in place while the adult consciousness shapes cadence, selection, and pattern. Read the essay · Download the PDF

The Grammar of Interruption

Dialogue mechanics and withheld speech — action beats that do three jobs at once, and em-dashes reserved for pressure rather than routine punctuation. Read the essay · Download the PDF

The Religious Architecture of the Work

The full concordance study of religious allusion across the fiction and poetry — the recurring images, prayers, and readings collected in one place. Read the study · Download the PDF

Credo: A Statement of Belief

The artistic creed — writing from mercy rather than judgment, each story a small act of faith. Read the credo · Download the PDF

All We Ever Needed: The Architecture of Mercy

A study of the short-story collection — mercy as its governing ethic, carried by gesture rather than argued onto the page. Read the essay · Download the PDF

Essays — Criticism & Craft: Book Club Companion

A single companion gathering the essays for reading groups and the classroom. Browse the essays · Download the PDF
