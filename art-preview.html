Four small paintings for a literary site

These original illustrated panels are intended as occasional breathing spaces between pages and passages: warm, architectural, and readable rather than dark or decorative.

Morning light, manuscripts, coffee, and a room made for sentences.

Bookshop at Evening

A single warm window holding its own against the blue street.

After the Reading

Empty tables, amber pendants, and the afterglow of a public room.

Platform at Dusk

A pale sky, a suitcase, and the small expectancy of departure.
