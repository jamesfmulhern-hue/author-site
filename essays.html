Essays

Short critical and personal essays on the recurring images, religious interpretation, governing methods, and working beliefs behind the fiction, poetry, and teaching of James Mulhern.

An Editor’s Note

On this series

These six essays read James Mulhern’s work the way a careful reader would — following a single image or conviction across stories, poems, and the classroom until it declares its own logic. Four are close readings: recurring imagery in fire and water, the sacred hidden inside shabby, ordinary detail, and a scholarly reading of the Assumption of Mary as a key to “Assumptions.” Two are the author’s own account of what he believes writing and teaching are for.
